Things I've built

Fulude's Cafe: restaurant e-commerce and admin platform

Group Leader & Project Manager Team of 4 200+ commits on GitHub Live in production

A complete online ordering platform for Fulude's Cafe, a restaurant in Mapate, Thohoyandou. Customers browse the menu, order for collection, delivery or dine-in, pay by card and book tables, while the owner runs the whole business from a private admin dashboard covering orders, stock, reservations, reviews and reports.

Visit live site View code on GitHub

What the platform does

Customer site
  • Online ordering for collection, delivery (fee by distance) or dine-in, with spice levels, sides and extras, and a cart saved to the customer's account.
  • Secure card payments through Yoco, with automatic refunds when an order is cancelled.
  • Table reservations with optional pre-ordered meals, confirmed automatically once paid.
  • Accounts and reviews with email or Google sign-in, order history and moderated customer reviews.
  • Buffet seat booking, catering enquiries, and an installable app with push notifications.
Admin site
  • Live dashboard with today's orders and revenue, average order value, six-month charts and top customers.
  • Live order board for the kitchen with sound alerts, plus a separate staff portal.
  • Menu and stock control with low-stock flags, stock-up suggestions and timed specials.
  • Reservations, reviews and reports: confirm or cancel bookings, approve and reply to reviews, sales reports and an audit log.
  • Staff and admin accounts with role-based permissions.

How I led the team

  • Planned and divided the work into three tracks (customer site, backend and admin) so four developers could build in parallel.
  • Collaborated through GitHub: 200+ commits across separate feature branches for the customer site, backend, admin and delivery, merged into main.
  • Protected the codebase: a pre-commit hook scans for leaked secrets before anything is pushed.
  • Shipped to a live domain on Cloudflare Pages with a PostgreSQL database, then kept improving it after launch.
  • Stayed hands-on in the code across the admin dashboard, reservations and customer site, not just managing from the side.
